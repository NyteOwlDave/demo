----------------------------------------------------------------

$$
\rho(\theta) = \frac{\pi}{2}e^\frac{-\theta}{8\pi}
$$

$$
X(\theta) = 1 - \frac{1}{2}( \frac{5}{4}( 1 - \frac{\theta \mod {2\pi}}{\pi} )^2 - \frac{1}{4} )^2
$$

$$
{( x,y )} = (r\sin{\theta}, r\cos{\theta})
$$

$$
{( z )} = X(x\cos{\rho} - y\sin{\rho})
$$
