## Eq. 1

$$
\frac{1}{4} \pi (9cm)^2 = \frac{81}{4} \pi cm^2
$$

----------------------------------------------------------------

## Eq. 2

$$
\frac {5}{4} \pi (3cm)^2 = \frac {45}{4} \pi cm^2
$$

----------------------------------------------------------------

## Eq. 3

$$
\frac {81}{4} \pi - \frac {45}{4} \pi = \frac {81-45} {4} \pi = \frac {36}{4} \pi = 9 \pi
$$

----------------------------------------------------------------

## Eq. 4

$$
\frac {81}{4} \pi cm^2 - \frac {45}{4} \pi cm^2 = 9 \pi cm^2
$$
