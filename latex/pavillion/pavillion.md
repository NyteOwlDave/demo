## Parametric Particle Equation

----------------------------------------------------------------

> This is the original equation, discovered on FB Reels. It 
> draws a __particle mesh__ with animated waves.

----------------------------------------------------------------

- `t` is time (sec)
- `g` is gravitation acceleration (m/sec^2)
- `h` is resting height of particle plane

----------------------------------------------------------------

$$
z(t) = h - 0.5 \cdot g \cdot t^2
$$
