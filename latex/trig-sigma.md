# Trig Function Sigma Notation

----------------------------------------------------------------

$$
sin(x) = \Sigma_{n=0}^{\infty}
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\alpha} {\Delta}
\end{bmatrix} \kappa
$$
$$ \alpha = (-1)^n $$
$$ \Delta = (2n - 1)! $$
$$ \kappa = x^{2n+1} $$

----------------------------------------------------------------

$$
cos(x) = \Sigma_{n=0}^{\infty}
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\alpha} {\Delta}
\end{bmatrix} \kappa
$$
$$ \alpha = (-1)^n $$
$$ \Delta = (2n)! $$
$$ \kappa = x^{2n} $$
